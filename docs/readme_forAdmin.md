# TutorLink - Administrator User Manual (`readme_forAdmin.md`)

## Overview
As a TutorLink Administrator, you have full oversight across all platform operations, including student and tutor account management, match requests, scheduled tutoring sessions, GCash payment logs, subjects catalog configuration, broadcast notifications, and activity reporting.

---

## Admin Capabilities

### 1. Dashboard KPIs
- View system-wide key performance indicators: Total Registered Students, Total Tutors, Total Tutoring Volume (₱), and Platform Net Revenue (10% commission).

### 2. Students Management
- Monitor student registrations, grade levels, and completed session totals.
- Activate or deactivate student accounts.

### 3. Tutors Management
- View registered tutors, hourly rates, overall ratings, review counts, and status.
- Activate or deactivate tutor accounts.

### 4. Tutor Match Requests
- Review student-tutor match requests and AI match compatibility scores.
- Approve, cancel, or reassign match requests.

### 5. Scheduled Sessions Ledger
- Monitor scheduled tutoring sessions, session dates, time slots, and status (`Confirmed`, `In Progress`, `Completed`, `Cancelled`).
- Cancel sessions or process manual reschedule requests when necessary.

### 6. Payments & Payouts Log
- Review GCash payment transaction reference numbers, amounts, and payout statuses (`Unpaid` vs `Paid Out`).
- Confirm payments and mark payouts as `Paid Out` once sessions are completed.

### 7. Subjects Catalog
- Add new subject categories or rename/delete subjects.

### 8. Broadcast Notifications & Reports
- Send system broadcast messages to all users or target roles.
- Filter session activity by date range, tutor, or subject, and export formula-safe CSV reports.
